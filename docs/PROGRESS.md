## Why the mechanism can change without improving the winner

In the fixed-query cohort, harness performed 30 promotions: 16 at attempt 8 and 14 at the final attempt 12. The latter cannot affect a future proposal. Multiagent reproduction occurred at attempt 10, leaving only two proposals; in 25 of 30 runs the final winner had already been found by that point. Weight adaptation changed all 30 policies, but only nine paired runs changed their sampled trajectories and none changed their final winner.

These observations motivate a future, separately registered study with more room for adaptation. They do not justify changing the current budget after inspecting the confirmation split. The H200 measurements should first follow the registered protocol.
